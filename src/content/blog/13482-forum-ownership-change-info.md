<p>As of 6/11/2020 I have transferred my ownership of the forum to [mention]nonprofit[/mention] , an account owned by The East Pacific Community Corporation, which is a nonprofit that has been created that will be dedicated to managing all of The East Pacific off-site OOC properties.</p>
<p>Previously, I had ownership of the forum, tep.wiki, tep.li, twitter, and Gmail/YouTube, but those have all now been transferred to the The East Pacific Community Corporation. The Board of Directors currently consists of Pax, Stooley and myself.</p>
<p>A Few Notes:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><a href="http://TheEastPacific.com">TheEastPacific.com</a> will be transferred to the nonprofit at a later date.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>We’re looking into transferring all official Discord ownerships to a TEPCC account</p>
</li>
</ul>
