<p>As an administrative decision, effectively immediately, all official TEP Discord servers shall be owned by a TEP Forum Admin. Any server not under their (the Administrators and Global Moderators listed on <a href="https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/the_east_pacific/memberlist.php?mode=team" class="inline-onebox">The East Pacific - Tapatalk - The team</a>) direct control shall be considered non-affiliated with The East Pacific. This is in the best interest of for all members of TEP and to better manage and moderate these servers.</p>
<p>-The East Pacific Administrative Team</p>
